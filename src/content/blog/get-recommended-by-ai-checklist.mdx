You do not need a big budget to improve how AI assistants talk about you. Work through this list in order, and tick things off as you go.

## 1. Make your own site easy to understand

- [ ] Your homepage says in one sentence what you do and who it is for
- [ ] You have a clear page for each product or service
- [ ] You have an About page with real names, places and facts
- [ ] Your pricing or "how it works" is written in plain text, not only inside images

## 2. Keep your facts consistent

- [ ] Your business name is written the same way everywhere
- [ ] Your description matches across your site, social profiles and directories
- [ ] Contact details and links are current

## 3. Earn mentions you do not control

- [ ] Ask happy customers for reviews on the sites your buyers actually use
- [ ] Get listed in the directories and roundups for your category
- [ ] Contribute to communities where your audience asks questions

## 4. Answer real questions

| Question type | Example | Where to answer it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Comparison | "X vs Y for small teams" | A comparison page |
| How-to | "How do I set up Z?" | A guide or help article |
| Buying | "Best tool for agencies" | Your homepage and case studies |

<Callout title="Re-check regularly" tone="orange">
  Assistants change their answers over time. Re-run your key questions every month and note what moves.
</Callout>
