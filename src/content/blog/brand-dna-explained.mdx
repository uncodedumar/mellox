Ask any AI tool for a LinkedIn post and you will get something competent, polite and forgettable. The problem is not the model. It is that the model has never met your brand.

**Brand DNA** is the fix. It is a short, structured profile of your brand that Mellox builds from your website and keeps up to date, so every post, image and article starts from *who you are* instead of from a blank page.

## What goes into it

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Col>
    #### Voice
    How you talk: formal or friendly, short or detailed, the words you use and the ones you avoid.
  </Col>
  <Col>
    #### Audience
    Who you are speaking to, what they care about and what they already know.
  </Col>
  <Col>
    #### Look
    Colours, imagery and style, so visuals match your site and not a stock template.
  </Col>
</Columns>

## Why it changes the output

When the profile is in place, a request as simple as "write a post about our new feature" comes back with your tone, your audience in mind and your visual style, ready to review rather than rewrite.

<Quote by="What good output feels like">
  It should read like someone on your team wrote it on a good day.
</Quote>

## You stay in control

Brand DNA is never a black box. You can read it, edit any line, and choose per request whether to use it.

<Callout title="Tip" tone="blue">
  In the Mellox app you can pick **Brand DNA only** when you want strictly on-brand output, or loosen it when you want something more experimental.
</Callout>
