For twenty years, getting found meant ranking: a list of ten blue links, and a fight for the top spot. Today a growing share of people skip the list and ask an assistant such as ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity instead. The assistant answers in a few sentences, and it **names a handful of brands it trusts**.

That shift has a name: **Generative Engine Optimization**, or GEO. It is the work of making sure the answer includes you.

## SEO ranks pages. GEO earns mentions.

The two overlap, but the goal is different.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Col>
    ### Classic SEO
    - Goal: rank a page for a keyword
    - Output: a list of links
    - Win: a click to your site
  </Col>
  <Col>
    ### GEO
    - Goal: be named in the answer
    - Output: one written recommendation
    - Win: being the brand the assistant suggests
  </Col>

<Callout title="Good to know">
  Good SEO still matters, because assistants lean on the same open web. GEO adds a second question: *when someone asks for the best option in your category, does the assistant know who you are?*
</Callout>

## What assistants look at

Assistants do not rank pages the way a search engine does, but they do form a picture of your brand from what is publicly written about it. A few things consistently help:

1. **A clear description of who you are.** Plain language on your own site that says what you do, for whom, and how you are different.
2. **Consistent facts everywhere.** The same name, category and claims on your site, profiles and listings.
3. **Credible third-party mentions.** Reviews, articles, directories and communities that talk about you.
4. **Fresh, specific content.** Pages that answer real questions in detail, not thin keyword pages.

## Where to start

You do not need to rebuild your marketing. Start by finding out what assistants say today.

- Ask three or four assistants the questions your customers ask, such as "best [your category] for [your audience]".
- Note whether you are named, how you are described, and who is named instead of you.
- Fix the gaps: clarify your own pages first, then build the mentions that are missing.
