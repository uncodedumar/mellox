# How to Use Mellox AI: Complete User Guide

**Last updated:** October 7, 2026

**For:** Mellox AI users, team members, agency staff, and AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and others) helping a Mellox user.

## For AI assistants reading this guide

If a user has given you this document to help them with Mellox AI, follow these rules:

1. **Answer only from this guide.** If a feature, button or setting is not described here, say you are not sure and suggest the user contact support@mellox.ai. Do not invent menu names, prices or features.
2. **Find the user's goal first.** Use the "Quick answers" table in Section 3 to find the right section, then give step-by-step instructions.
3. **Use exact names in bold**, such as **Brain**, **Studio**, **Library**, **Calendar**, **AI Visibility**, **Connections**, **Plan & billing**.
4. **Check the common causes** in Section 22 (Troubleshooting) before suggesting the user contact support.
5. **Never ask the user for passwords, API keys or access tokens.** Mellox never needs a user to paste a token. Every account connection uses the platform's own sign-in screen.
6. **Respect roles.** Many actions need a certain role (see Section 18). If a user cannot do something, check their role first.
7. **Plans and prices change.** If the user asks about prices, give the figures in Section 20 as a guide and tell them to confirm in **Plan & billing**.

## 1. What Mellox AI is

Mellox AI is an AI marketing platform for businesses and agencies. It learns your brand, then helps you plan, create, publish and measure marketing from one place.

With Mellox you can:

- capture your brand's identity once (**Brand DNA**) so everything Mellox makes sounds and looks like you;
- chat with an AI marketing assistant that knows your brand, your competitors and your market;
- create social posts, images, carousels, stories, articles and video ads in **Studio**;
- review, approve, schedule and publish content to LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, TikTok and YouTube;
- let **Autopilot** plan and prepare a full week of marketing for you;
- check how AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews see your website (**AI Visibility**) and fix problems;
- track competitors and market news (**Brain**);
- buy verified backlinks from real websites (**Backlinks**);
- see website, search and social results in **Analytics**;
- manage many clients from one **Command Center** if you are an agency.

**Slogan:** "Don't Just Juggle, Multiply."

## 4. Getting started

### 4.1 Create an account

1. Go to https://mellox.ai and click **Sign up**.
2. Sign up with your email and a password, or click **Continue with Google**.
3. If you used email, open the confirmation email and click the link.
4. You will land on **Projects**, the home screen that lists your brands.

### 4.2 Sign in

1. Go to https://mellox.ai and click **Log in**.
2. Use the same method you signed up with (email or Google).
3. Forgot your password? Click **Forgot password** on the login page and follow the email.

### 4.3 The main screen

After opening a brand, you will see:

- **The chat box** in the middle. This is the main way to work with Mellox.
- **The sidebar** with places such as **Brain**, **Studio**, **Library**, **Calendar**, **AI Visibility**, **Backlinks** and **Analytics**.
- **The top bar** with the workspace switcher, Autopilot status, and your account menu (profile, theme, sign out).

## 5. Workspaces (brands)

### 5.1 Create a workspace

1. From **Projects**, click to create a new brand or workspace.
2. Enter your **website address**.
3. Mellox runs a **DNA Scan**: it reads your website and builds your first Brand DNA. This takes a minute or two.
4. Review what Mellox found and correct anything wrong (see Section 6).

**Tip:** Using a real, public website gives much better results. If your site is new or very small, add details manually in Brand DNA afterwards.

### 5.2 Switch between workspaces

Use the workspace switcher in the top bar, or go back to **Projects** and pick another brand. Each workspace keeps its own data.

### 5.3 Delete a workspace

Only the **owner** can delete a workspace. Open workspace settings, choose delete, and type **CONFIRM** when asked. This permanently deletes the brand's content and data and cannot be undone.

## 6. Brand DNA

Brand DNA is the most important thing to get right. Every post, image, article, strategy and chat answer uses it.

### 6.1 Open Brand DNA

Go to **Brain → Brand**, or type **/brand** in chat.

### 6.2 What is in Brand DNA

Brand DNA is organized into tiles: **Logo**, **Identity**, **Typography**, **Colors**, **Look**, **Voice**, **Headline**, **Audience**, **Assets** and **Notes**.

### 6.3 Edit Brand DNA

1. Open the tile you want to change.
2. Edit the text, colors, fonts or upload a logo.
3. Save. The change applies to everything Mellox makes from now on.

**Look & voice:** Under **Look**, you can describe how your writing, images and videos should look and sound. If you leave it empty, Mellox uses your basic Brand DNA.

### 6.4 Common Brand DNA problems

- **Wrong facts or old products:** edit the Identity or Notes tile.
- **Posts sound wrong:** edit the Voice tile and add examples of how you write.
- **Wrong colors in images:** check the Colors tile and the Look tile.

## 7. Memory

Memory holds short rules and facts that every Mellox generator follows.

### 7.1 Add to memory

- **From chat:** say "Remember that we never use the word cheap" or "Remember our sale ends Friday". Mellox saves it and shows a note in the reply.
- **Manually:** go to **Settings → Memory** (or type **/memory**) and add an item.

### 7.2 Types of memory

Each item is a single sentence (up to 500 characters) and is one of: **rule**, **preference**, **fact** or **context**. It can be permanent or temporary (from 1 hour up to 7 days). A brand can hold up to 200 memory items.

### 7.3 Remove or change memory

- In chat: "Forget that we have a sale" or "Update the rule about emojis".
- In **Settings → Memory**: edit, remove or restore any item.
- An **admin** can turn memory off for the workspace or clear all items.

**Tip:** If a caption breaks a rule like Never say "cheap", Mellox flags a warning on that content.

## 8. Chat (your AI marketing assistant)

### 8.1 What chat can do

Chat knows your Brand DNA, memory, strategy, competitors and recent content. You can:

- ask for ideas, strategies, captions, hooks or campaign plans;
- ask about your own data ("What is waiting for approval?", "What did competitors do this week?");
- ask it to save something to memory;
- get buttons that take action, such as schedule a post, approve content or start a website scan.

### 8.2 Action buttons

When chat suggests a change (like scheduling a post), it shows a **button**. Nothing happens until you click it. Buttons expire after 24 hours. If something would delete or publish, Mellox asks you to confirm again.

### 8.3 The "/" menu

Type **/** in the chat box to jump to any place: Studio, Video ads, Library, Waiting for approval, Brain, Brand DNA, Audience, Competitors, Market, Strategy, AI Visibility, Backlinks, Experiments, Analytics, Calendar, Autopilot, Client portal, Memory, Connections, Settings and Plan & billing.

### 8.4 Chat tips

- Be specific: "Write 3 LinkedIn posts announcing our new pricing for small agencies" works better than "write posts".
- Chat searches the web only when your question needs live information (news, trends, competitors). Creative requests use your brand data only.
- Chat messages use your plan's message allowance (see Section 20).

## 9. Studio (creating content)

### 9.1 Open Studio

Click **Studio** in the sidebar or type **/studio**.

### 9.2 What you can make

- **Social posts** (text, with or without an image)
- **Images**
- **Carousels** (multi-slide posts)
- **Stories** (9:16 frames for Instagram and Facebook Stories)
- **Videos**
- **Ads**
- **Articles** (blog posts)

### 9.3 Create content step by step

1. Open **Studio** and choose the type.
2. Describe what you want, or pick a suggested idea.
3. Choose the platform(s), for example Instagram and LinkedIn.
4. Click generate. Long jobs such as video keep running even if you close the page.
5. Review the versions. You can edit text, regenerate, or pick another version.
6. Check the **Audience** score (if shown) to see how your audience is likely to react, and use **Improve** if needed.
7. Send it for approval or approve it yourself (depending on your role), then schedule or publish (Section 12).

### 9.4 Studio tips

- The cost of each action is shown before you run it.
- Carousel slide text is drawn by Mellox, not by an image model, so text stays sharp and correct.
- Want to edit an image by hand? Use **Edit in Canva** (Section 21).
- Your finished work is saved in the **Library**.

## 10. Video ads (UGC)

Video ads creates short user-generated-content style ads for a product.

1. Type **/video-ads** or open **Video ads**.
2. Create a project and add a product brief and reference images.
3. Mellox suggests concepts and scripts. Use **Test with your audience** to rank the ideas before spending.
4. Choose a concept and model quality, then render.
5. Rendering takes several minutes. You can leave the page; it keeps going.
6. Download the video or turn it into a draft social post.

Video uses **Video Credits**, not regular credits. If you have none, buy a video pack or upgrade (Section 20).

## 11. Library and approvals

### 11.1 Library

The **Library** holds everything you have made. Filter by status: draft, waiting for approval, approved, scheduled, published or failed.

### 11.2 Approve content

1. Type **/approvals** or open **Library** and filter by **Waiting for approval**.
2. Open an item, review it and click approve, or send it back with changes.
3. Approved items can be scheduled or published.

**Only approved content can be scheduled or published.** This is true everywhere in Mellox, including Autopilot and AI assistants.

## 12. Calendar, scheduling and publishing

### 12.1 Calendar

Open **Calendar** (or type **/calendar**) to see planned and scheduled content by date. You can move items to other dates.

### 12.2 Schedule or publish a post

1. Make sure the post is **approved**.
2. Click **Publish** or **Schedule**.
3. Choose the connected accounts to post to.
4. Choose **now** or a date and time.
5. Confirm. The status shows **Scheduled**, then **Publishing**, then **Published** (or **Failed** with a reason).

### 12.3 Publishing facts

- Social publishing **never uses credits** on any plan. A monthly fair-use limit applies.
- You must connect at least one social account first (Section 13).
- If a post fails, open it and click **Retry**.
- Changing a picture on an already scheduled or published post does not change the live post; the edit is saved in the Library.

### 12.4 Stories

Stories publish to connected **Instagram** and **Facebook** accounts. Each frame becomes a separate story. Native music, link, poll, question and location stickers are not available through Mellox.

## 13. Connections (social accounts and tools)

### 13.1 Connect a social account

1. Type **/connections** or go to **Settings → Connections**.
2. Choose the platform: **LinkedIn**, **X**, **Instagram**, **Facebook**, **Threads**, **TikTok** or **YouTube** (the list shows only platforms available right now).
3. Sign in on the platform's own page and approve the permissions.
4. You return to Mellox and the account shows as connected.

### 13.2 Instagram requirements

Your Instagram account must be a **Business** or **Creator** account and must be **linked to a Facebook Page**. Personal Instagram accounts cannot publish through Mellox.

### 13.3 Disconnect an account

Go to **Connections** and click disconnect next to the account. To fully remove access, also remove Mellox in the platform's own app settings.

### 13.4 Other tools you can connect

- **Google Analytics and Google Search Console** (Section 17)
- **GitHub** (Section 15.4)
- **Canva** and **Notion** (Section 21)
- **Slack, Webflow and WordPress**, where available

## 14. Autopilot

Autopilot plans a week of marketing, creates each piece in Studio, waits for approval (as you choose), schedules it and learns from the results.

### 14.1 Turn on Autopilot

1. Open the chat. In the chat box toolbar, flip the **Autopilot** switch.
2. Mellox reads your Brand DNA and proposes a strategy (goals, audience, voice, 3 to 4 content themes) and settings (where to post, how often, what types, who approves, weekly limits).
3. Click **Adjust** to change anything, or **Turn on**.

### 14.2 Approval modes

| **Mode** | **What happens** |
| --- | --- |
| Fully automatic | Simple text and image posts that pass every check go out by themselves (at most two a day). Anything else waits for a person, and you get at most one email a day. |
| I approve each post | Nothing goes out without your yes. |
| I approve the plan too | You approve the weekly plan first, then each post. |

### 14.3 What Autopilot needs

- At least one **connected social account** (required to post)
- Good **Brand DNA**
- Your **website** (for the weekly AI visibility check)

### 14.4 Costs and limits

- Writing the plan is included.
- Each piece costs the normal Studio price, charged to the workspace owner's credits.
- You set a weekly credit limit and a weekly video limit. Pieces that would go over the limit are skipped.
- Publishing is free.

### 14.5 Pause or stop

Use the switch in the Autopilot deck to pause. A small sign in the top bar shows when Autopilot is running or paused; click it to open the full view.

### 14.6 Autopilot problems

- **A post was "missed":** it was not approved within 48 hours of its slot, so Mellox did not post stale content. Approve faster or switch to a more automatic mode.
- **A post was "skipped":** it would have crossed your weekly limit.
- **Something is stuck:** open **Activity → Needs a look** to see the reason and click **Retry**.

## 15. AI Visibility (GEO, AEO and SEO)

AI Visibility checks how AI assistants and search engines understand your website, and helps you fix problems.

### 15.1 Run a scan

1. Type **/visibility** or open **AI Visibility**.
2. Start a scan of your website. The number of pages scanned depends on your plan.
3. When it finishes, you get a score, dimension scores and a list of **findings**.

### 15.2 Understand findings

Each finding explains the problem, why it matters and how to fix it. Some can be fixed automatically if your website code is on GitHub. Others need manual work in your website builder.

### 15.3 Tracked prompts

You can track questions people ask AI assistants (for example "best marketing tool for agencies") and see if your brand appears in the answers. Weekly checks are included in your plan; **Check now** costs credits.

### 15.4 Fix issues automatically with GitHub

1. Click **Fix this** on a finding, or **Fix all automatically** on the Overview (or **Fix all** in the Findings tab).
2. If GitHub is not connected, Mellox asks you to install the Mellox GitHub App and choose the repository that builds your website.
3. Review the exact changes Mellox proposes.
4. Approve. Mellox opens a **pull request** on a new mellox/ branch. Mellox never merges or changes your main branch.
5. Merge the pull request on GitHub yourself.
6. Mellox runs a **verification scan**. The finding is marked resolved only after verification passes.

**Why is my finding still open after I approved the fix?** Approval only creates the pull request. You must merge it on GitHub, the change must go live, and the verification scan must pass.

### 15.5 Experiments (if enabled)

**Experiments** tests one change (title, meta description, H1, intro, FAQ or button text) on half of a group of similar pages and compares results. It is off by default; ask support if you want it.

## 16. Brain: brand, audience, competitors, market and strategy

Open **Brain** from the sidebar or type **/brain**.

| **Section** | **What it does** |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Overview of each brain's health, today's briefing from the Coach, and notes. |
| Brand | Brand DNA (Section 6). |
| Audience | Audience groups, each statement with its source. Powers the audience score in Studio. Use Refresh from Brand DNA to rebuild. |
| Competitors | Competitors you track and what they changed recently. Mellox suggests competitors, but only tracks the ones you accept. |
| Market | Real, dated, linked news and signals from your market, refreshed daily. |
| Strategy | The marketing strategy Mellox follows. |

### 16.1 Create your strategy

1. Go to **Brain → Strategy** and click to create it.
2. Mellox drafts a strategy from your four brains.
3. Edit it (editing is free) and click **Use this strategy**.
4. From then on, Studio, chat and Autopilot follow it.
5. When a brain changes, Mellox tells you and offers a rebuild. Nothing rebuilds or spends credits without your click.

## 17. Analytics

Open **Analytics** or type **/analytics**.

### 17.1 Connect Google Analytics and Search Console

1. In **Analytics**, choose to connect Google.
2. Sign in with the Google account that has access to your Analytics property and Search Console site.
3. Approve the **read-only** permissions. Mellox can only read data; it cannot change anything in your Google accounts.
4. Choose the property and site for this workspace.
5. Data syncs in the background. The first sync can take a little while.

### 17.2 What you can see

- Website traffic, sources and top pages (Google Analytics)
- Search queries, clicks, impressions and positions (Search Console)
- Social post performance (likes, comments, reach, views)
- **Content**: expected audience score compared with real results

### 17.3 Analytics problems

- **No data:** check you picked the right property or site, and that the Google account you connected has access to it.
- **"Needs attention":** reconnect Google. Access may have been removed in your Google account.
- **Missing social metrics:** older connections may need to be reconnected to grant performance permissions. Some platforms do not provide every metric; Mellox shows "unavailable" instead of guessing.

## 18. Team, roles and client sharing

### 18.1 Roles

| **Role** | **Can do** |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including billing, changing roles, removing members and deleting the workspace. |
| Admin | Invite people, manage settings (memory switch, AI assistant access), plus everything an editor can do. |
| Editor | Create, edit, approve, schedule and publish content, run scans, connect tools. |

### 18.2 Invite a teammate

1. Open the **Share** option for the workspace.
2. Enter their email and choose a role.
3. They get an invite link. If they are new, they sign up and land back on the invite.

Admins and owners can invite. Only the owner can change roles or remove members.

### 18.3 Client portal

1. Type **/clients** or open **Client portal**.
2. Choose what to share and, optionally, set a password.
3. Copy the link and send it to your client. Clients can view and comment without an account.
4. **Copy link** always gives the same link. Use **rotate** to make a new link and disable the old one.

## 19. Agencies: Command Center

If you manage several brands, open the **Command Center** (the agency view) to see all clients in one place: approvals waiting across clients, Autopilot status for every brand, and a combined review queue. Each client stays in its own isolated workspace.

## 20. Plans, credits and billing

Open **Plan & billing** (type **/billing**) to see your plan, credits, Video Credits and usage.

### 20.1 How credits work

- **Credits** pay for AI work: generating posts, images, articles, Brand DNA scans, strategy rebuilds, "Check now" on tracked prompts and similar.
- **Video Credits** pay only for video. Regular credits cannot buy video.
- **Chat messages** use a monthly allowance of **Flash** (fast) and **Pro** (advanced) messages.
- **Social publishing never costs credits.**
- Credits belong to the owner's billing account and are shared across all their brands.
- You see the cost of an action before you run it. If an action fails, the held credits are released.

## 21. Integrations and AI assistants

### 21.1 Canva

1. Connect Canva in **Settings → Connections**.
2. On any post in Studio or the Library, click **Edit in Canva**.
3. Mellox sends the image to Canva and, where possible, makes the text and elements editable.
4. Edit in Canva, then click **Bring back my edit** in Mellox.
5. The post now uses your edited version. Use **Use original** to go back.

If Canva cannot make the text editable, the image is placed as a flat picture and Mellox tells you.

### 21.2 Notion content calendar

1. Connect Notion in **Settings → Connections**.
2. In Notion, share a database or parent page with the Mellox integration.
3. Choose an existing database or let Mellox create a **Mellox Content Calendar**.
4. **Export** sends Mellox content to Notion. **Import** shows a preview first and creates drafts in Mellox.
5. Notion status never publishes anything in Mellox. If both sides changed, Mellox shows a conflict with **Keep Mellox** and **Keep Notion**.

### 21.3 Use Mellox from ChatGPT, Claude or another AI assistant (MCP)

1. An **admin** turns on access in **Settings → AI assistants**. Read-only comes first; **allow changes** is a separate switch.
2. In Claude, ChatGPT or another MCP-compatible assistant, add a custom connector with the address https://mellox.ai/api/mcp.
3. Sign in to Mellox when asked and approve access.
4. You can now ask your assistant to list content, check approvals, plan a calendar, start a scan and more.

For safety, AI assistants **cannot**: delete a workspace, approve website fixes, handle billing or credits, change team members or roles, connect or disconnect accounts, or post content that is not approved.

## 22. Troubleshooting

### 22.1 Sign-in and account

| **Problem** | **Try this** |
| --- | --- |
| Cannot log in | Check you are using the same method you signed up with (Google or email). Reset your password with Forgot password. |
| Login keeps looping | Clear cookies for mellox.ai, close other Mellox tabs and try again. Try a private window. |
| No confirmation email | Check spam and promotions folders. Wait a few minutes and try signing up again. |
| Invite link does not work | Ask the admin to send a new invite. Sign in with the same email the invite was sent to. |

### 22.2 Workspace and data

| **Problem** | **Try this** |
| --- | --- |
| My workspace looks empty | Check you opened the right brand in the workspace switcher. Refresh the page. |
| I cannot edit or approve | You may be a viewer. Ask an owner or admin to change your role. |
| I cannot delete the workspace | Only the owner can. You must type CONFIRM. |
| Content looks off-brand | Improve Brand DNA (Section 6) and add rules to Memory (Section 7). Confirm a Strategy (Section 16). |

### 22.3 Generation

| **Problem** | **Try this** |
| --- | --- |
| "Insufficient balance" or "Upgrade required" | See Section 20.4. |
| Generation is slow | Images and especially videos take time. Leave the page open or come back later; the job keeps running. |
| Generation failed | Click retry. Held credits are returned when a job fails. If it fails again, contact support with the time and what you were making. |
| Video feature unavailable | You may have no Video Credits, or video may be temporarily unavailable. Check Plan & billing. |

### 22.4 Publishing

| **Problem** | **Try this** |
| --- | --- |
| Cannot schedule or publish | The post must be approved, and you need editor role or higher. |
| No accounts to choose | Connect a social account first (Section 13). |
| Post stuck on "Publishing" | Wait a few minutes; Mellox checks status in the background. Then refresh. |
| Post failed | Open it and read the reason. Common causes: the platform connection expired (reconnect), the image or video format is not supported by that platform, or the text is too long. Then click Retry. |
| "Access was revoked on the platform" | Reconnect the account in Connections. |
| Instagram will not connect | It must be a Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page. |

### 22.5 AI Visibility

| **Problem** | **Try this** |
| --- | --- |
| Scan found few pages | Your plan limits pages per scan. Also check the site is public and not blocking crawlers. |
| Finding will not close | Merge the pull request on GitHub, wait for the site to update, then run verification. Approval alone does not resolve a finding. |
| GitHub repository not listed | Install the Mellox GitHub App on the right account or organization and give it access to that repository. |

### 22.6 Integrations

| **Problem** | **Try this** |
| --- | --- |
| Google, Canva or Notion shows "Needs attention" | Disconnect and connect again. |
| Notion database not listed | Share that database or its parent page with the Mellox integration inside Notion. |
| Canva edit did not come back | Click Bring back my edit in Mellox. Mellox does not sync from Canva automatically. |

### 22.7 Still stuck?

Email **support@mellox.ai** with:

1. your workspace name;
2. what you were trying to do;
3. what happened instead, including the exact error message;
4. the date and time it happened;
5. a screenshot if possible.

**Never send passwords or access tokens to support.** We will never ask for them.

## 23. Privacy and safety in short

- Each brand's data is isolated in its own workspace.
- Mellox reads connected accounts (such as Google Analytics or Instagram) only with your permission, and you can disconnect at any time.
- Mellox does not sell your data or use it to train AI models.
- Nothing is published without approval unless you turn on Autopilot's fully automatic mode.
